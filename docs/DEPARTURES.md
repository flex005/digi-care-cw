# Departures from the Care Worker PRD

Where this build does something other than `docs/CW_PRD.md` says, it is written here with the reason. Each was decided, not missed. A developer reading a screen that disagrees with the PRD should find it here; if it is not here, the screen is wrong.

## Scope

- **Care Home only.** The PRD's Hospital (Acute and Community), Domiciliary Care and Supported Living variations (rows 10b–10d, 13a, 15a–15b, 16a, 20a) are not built.
- **Biometric sign-in, GPS check-in and shared-device mode are stated as unavailable, not drawn** (AUTH-06, PROF-01 device settings). They depend on a device and a service this build does not have, and a drawn toggle would imply a behaviour nothing performs.
- **Reports (RPT-01) is not built.** Neither role has access, so the navigation holds no Reports item and there is no address: RPT-01 is in the CW PRD as a design reference for a screen the Admin build owns, not as a screen this product builds. (This line said "its address shows a no-access page" until Phase 10 checked; no such page was ever built, and a restricted-access screen at an address nobody can reach would be a screen telling somebody about a module they were never shown.)

## Decisions carried from the Admin & Manager build

The two products describe one home. Where the Admin build already decided something the PRD asks for differently, this build follows the Admin build, for the Admin build's reasons.

- **Eight consent types, the fixtures' eight** (CON-01). The PRD lists Medical Treatment, Family Portal Access, Photography, Sharing information with GP, Sharing information with social care, Financial decisions, Research participation, and Recording and monitoring. The fixtures hold Care and Support, Medication Administration, Photography and Video, Data Sharing, Family Portal Access, Research and Audit, Medical Treatment, and Electronic Records. Four names overlap. **The consent list is a fact about the home rather than a screen decision**, and the manager screens already show these eight; a care worker screen showing a different eight would leave a developer to work out which is true.
- **The password strength bar reads "N of 5 rules met"**, not Weak/Fair/Strong in red, amber and green (AUTH-02). Traffic-light colours are for findings, and "strong" is a claim about security nothing in this build can make.
- **The active sessions list has one row: this session** (PROF-01, AUTH-09). A list of other devices would be invented records.
- **No confirmation claims anybody was notified.** "Incident reported. Your manager has been notified." (INC-03) and every push the PRD describes are replaced by a statement that nothing was sent, at the point of the act. Somebody who believes the manager was told may not telephone the manager.
- **Amber is for findings, never for a gap or a notice.** The PRD's amber dot on a profile tab for "GP not recorded" or a risk never assessed (RES-03) is words on the tab, hatched where they name a gap (see Resident record, below). A PRN administration on the MAR (MED-04) is not amber: it is a recorded dose. Partial attendance on an activity card (ACT-01) renders as two facts, the attendance recorded and the remainder not recorded, rather than one amber card. A goal's "Not yet asked" (GOAL-02) is the hatch. An escalated omission (MED-01) is two facts, not one amber, hatched, dashed pill.
- **"No care note in 48h" at zero is a plain zero** (RES-01). The PRD hatches it. A zero there says every resident has a note, and a hatch would claim a gap the record says is not there.
- **No draft survives a sign-out** (AUTH-09, CN-02). Nothing in this build persists outside the tab, and record data is never written to browser storage, so the "unsaved care note" recovery banner on the next login has nothing to recover.
- **Round times are read-only** (DASH-01a says "configured by Admin"). Every medication record was produced against them.

## Rulings on the PRD

- **One straight bar per round, not a ring** (DASH-01, "Rounds today"). The hatch has to survive a Figma import, and a CSS gradient cannot follow a curve: the Admin build's ring tracks arrived blank. A ring whose meaning lives in a hatch that cannot be exported only works in the running build, and the running build is not the deliverable.
- **Start review and Re-score do not render for a care worker** (DASH-01 "Already late"). Neither role-table row lets a care worker do either, and a button that refuses on every row of a list is noise.
- **One harm vocabulary throughout**: No harm, Low harm, Moderate harm, Severe harm. The PRD's incident list badge "HIGH HARM" (INC-01) is not used; it is not one of the four the form records.
- **A care note's flag is placed by its author** (CN-02). The notifications table's "Your care note flagged by a senior" describes a different model and is not followed.
- **The medication PIN is called the medication PIN, and it also signs a handover and a risk assessment.** The PRD says the PIN is "used ONLY for medication confirmation" (AUTH-02) and then asks for it to sign a handover (HO-01) and a risk assessment (RA-02). Every screen that asks for it says what it is signing.
- **Add interim stays visible on Medications and refuses** (MED tabs), with the role table's reason: only a clinician or a manager adds an interim medication.
- **Pharmacy cycle is left out** of the Medications tabs. Neither role touches it and the PRD specifies no screen for it.
- **Senior carer acts the PRD names without a screen** (acknowledge an incident, record consent, upload a document, create an activity session, conduct a review) **adapt the Admin build's screens for the same acts**, narrowed to what the role table allows. They are the same acts with a narrower permission, and a second design would be two answers to one question.
- **A care worker sees the residents they were given, and that is scope, never blame.** "1 of your 9 residents" states what they can see. The assignment never decides a figure about a person.

## Care notes (Phase 3)

- **A flag is placed by the note's author, as they write it** (CN-02's toggle, CN-01's "flagging sends push to Senior on duty", and the cross-portal line). Table 19's "Your care note flagged by a senior" describes the other model and is not followed; its "Your flagged note reviewed" is, as a line saying nobody is notified. Recorded under Rulings above since the phase plan, and settled here.
- **A flag carries why, and a review carries what was done**, added to the shared type in both builds (CN-02 "Why are you flagging this?", CN-01 "Action taken?"). The reason is written or not given, and "No reason given" is stated, never an empty line. The outcome is one of four, with "Other" requiring words. Both are what somebody said: choosing "Incident raised" or "Care plan updated" raises nothing and edits nothing. Where that would be believed, the screen says so: "No incident is created here: this records that one was raised."
- **Only the author corrects a note, both roles** (the CW PRD says only that a note cannot be edited). A correction is a second note linked both ways; the original stays. The Admin build let anyone correct anything, by default rather than by decision. **A senior carer who believes a colleague's note is wrong** is told on the note: "Only C. Nwosu, who wrote it, can correct it. If you believe it is wrong, speak to C. Nwosu, or write your own note saying what you found", with a link to write one. They cannot flag somebody else's note under the flag model above. This is the first act in the role table whose source is a departure rather than a PRD row.
- **Voice-to-text is refused, not left unbuilt** (CN-02's mic button). The browser's built-in speech API sends the audio to a third party, which is no place for a care note. The button is drawn, disabled, with that sentence. A speech service a care provider could hold is a question for whoever builds the real product.
- **Mood is five words and an explicit "Not recorded"**, where the PRD draws five faces. A face alone is unreadable to a screen reader and ambiguous to everyone else. Nothing is chosen when the form opens, because a pre-chosen mood records one nobody observed.
- **The shift comes from the home's clock when the note is written**, changeable with a required reason (CN-02: "pre-filled from diGi-Time clock-in"). There is no clock-in to read.
- **Drafts are kept for the session, automatically, and said on return** ("Draft from 20:14 kept for this session."), with no dialog on navigating away and no "Save as draft" link (CN-02). Nothing is lost by leaving, so a dialog would guard nothing and teach people to dismiss dialogs. A draft is destroyed on sign-out and listed among what would go.
- **Phrase chips go in at the cursor** (CN-02), and are openers, never findings ("Ate ", "Declined "), as in the Admin build.
- **No goal-progress prompt until Phase 7** (CN-02). A prompt that links nothing would claim a link.
- **"Your notes", and no "By author" view or colleague picker** (CN-01). Reading a colleague's notes by their name is the blame the scope rule exists to stop, and a care worker has no reason to read by author that is not that.
- **"7 flagged notes, across your 4 residents", with the oldest wait**, not "7, of 11,205 on record" (CN-01). A ratio against every note ever written is a figure nobody acts on; the denominator that matters is how many people it concerns. It is the screen's one dark card.
- **Mark reviewed is drawn for a senior carer on each flagged row; a care worker gets one line at the head of the queue**, not a disabled button on every row (CN-01: "visible ONLY to Senior Carers").
- **A review can be undone for the rest of the session** by whoever recorded it. A mis-click that silently removed a request for help would be worse than an undo that expires.
- **Nobody is notified of a flag or a review**, and the screen says so at both acts (CN-01, CN-02 cross-portal pushes).

## Medications (Phase 4)

- **A care worker's controlled drug dose shows the contradiction in the role table, quoted, and Given stays unavailable.** "Medications — record Given/Not Given/PRN" gives a care worker "Can (PIN required)" and "Medications — countersign controlled drugs" says "Both must be Senior+". The build does not choose between them; see Questions for the PRD's author.
- **A senior carer records a controlled drug dose as Witness 1, and it is half a record until Witness 2 countersigns** on the register with their own PIN (MED-03). The missing signature is drawn beside "Given" as its own gap. The same person cannot be both witnesses: the screen says so, and the dose stays half-recorded rather than pretending to be finished.
- **A care worker's round shows the residents on their list, with a line saying why**: "Residents not on your list are not shown: the PRD does not say whether you record their doses." (MED-02; see Questions.)
- **The medication PIN is checked only where one was chosen this session** (MED-02). Five wrong lock it for fifteen minutes on the real clock. For anybody else the screen says first that no PIN is held, and any four digits confirm: the same honesty as the six-digit code.
- **"Resident vomiting" is a Not given reason** in the shared type, in both builds, as MED-02 lists it.
- **An omission can be closed, with who, when and why, and closing leaves the gap** (MED-01: "Cannot close or dismiss — Senior Carer or Manager required"). The closure is in the shared type, in both builds. A closed omission is still a dose with no record, and the cell stays hatched with the closure beside it.
- **The controlled drug register tab is drawn for a care worker and refuses**, with the role table's reason (MED-03: "Standard care workers cannot view this tab"). A hidden tab and a refused one say different things: the reader should know the register exists and is not theirs.
- **The PRN outcome reminder after 30 minutes is not sent** (MED-02). A PRN dose shows "Outcome not recorded yet" with a way to record it, and a line saying no reminder is sent. The 30-minute push and the 60-minute alert to the senior on duty are likewise not sent, and the round says so; a discrepancy's alert to the manager is not sent, and the register says so.
- **A round is recorded per resident, with one PIN confirming what it signs** (MED-02 asks for the PIN on each Given or PRN). The shared loader writes a resident's round as one act, so no round is left half-signed with some doses attributed and some not; the PIN step lists every dose and answer it confirms.
- **The completion banner does not ask "Record now or close?"** (MED-02). Closing a round records nothing; the banner states how many are recorded and how many are not.
- **Export as PDF is drawn and not built** (MED-04), and says no file is produced.
- **A controlled drug whose stock count does not reconcile cannot be given**, with MED-03's words at the dose: "Stock count does not match the running balance. This must be resolved before any further administration." **A departure from the Admin build**, whose round deliberately stopped blocking; the PRD says the discrepancy blocks further administration, and this product is the one at the trolley.
- **No PIN status per witness** (MED-03 asks for "Witness 1 + PIN status, Witness 2 + PIN status"). No record holds whether a signature was confirmed with a PIN, and a tick nothing stands behind would be the worst kind of reassurance. The register shows who signed and when; a missing second signature is its own gap.
- **A PRN dose is recorded at the prescribed dose** (MED-02 asks for "Dose given"). The shared record holds no dose of its own for a PRN administration, so the screen shows the prescribed dose and says a different one cannot be recorded here.
- **A dose cannot be recorded before its window opens, and the dose says so with the hour** (17/09/2026). MED-02 draws the round with its time windows and does not say a dose may only be answered inside one. Given and Not given are both unavailable before the window opens, with one line at the act: "This dose cannot be recorded before its window opens at 20:00 BST. Giving a drug early is a clinical decision, and nothing in this product can make one." **Both answers, not only Given**: recording that a resident refused a dose they have not yet been offered is a record of something that has not happened. **Recording late stays open**: a dose given at 20:40 is recorded at 20:40, and a window that closes with nothing in it is an omission, which the Omissions tab holds. The line is neutral information, never hatched and never a status colour: nothing is missing from the record, and nobody is being refused — the dose is not due. A round picked before it opens says so on each dose, at the foot of each card with the resident named, and on the banner.
- **The MAR chart draws nine states, where the PRD names five.** Its five are given, not given, PRN, no record and window open. The chart adds: a controlled drug given with no second signature (two facts, not one), an omission somebody has closed (still no record, with the closure beside it), a round before the prescription started, and a round this chart holds no cell for. Each is a different thing to read, and collapsing any of them into "no record" would say something untrue.

## Handover (Phase 5)

- **The board is every resident at the home, for both roles, and nothing on it is counted over the viewer's list** (HO-01: "Every resident at this site, whether or not anybody has got to them yet."). A handover is a property of the shift rather than of an assignment: the incoming shift takes the whole building. So the figures say what they are counted over and `scopeNote` is deliberately not used, since it would tell a care worker the board was counted over their list.
- **What is asked per resident is the act, not the row.** A care worker recording a status for a resident off their list draws the PRD's question at the control, as the other four "Can" rows do; residents on their list get the live control. A care worker nobody has given a list is told once, under the pills, rather than on every row.
- **Not reviewed leads, and it is the one dark card** (HO-01's first KPI). The other three figures are counted over the residents somebody reviewed, never over the home: counting the unreviewed in that denominator would claim a coverage nobody has. Each group's claim carries its own denominator for the same reason.
- **All four groups are always drawn, with their counts, even at zero** (HO-01's four tabs). They are filter pills over one list, not tabs: it is one board seen four ways.
- **All well is drawn quietly, not as a filled pill.** Twenty green rows would compete with the hatch on the one screen whose job is finding the residents nobody looked at. The author and the time are on the row either way.
- **A status of "needs attention" or "urgent" is refused without a note**, on the screen and in the loader (HO-01's inline note). A status telling the incoming shift somebody needs attention without saying what for is a signal with nothing behind it.
- **The signature is confirmed with the medication PIN**, which is what this build calls the code a person chooses at account setup, and it says in words what it signs, including how many residents nobody looked at. The signature stores those counts, so it can never be read as more than it was.
- **One signature is half a handover**, from the role table's completion (Table 3: "both shifts must sign"). The other half stays hatched until the incoming shift signs, and a care worker gets the table's refusal on both.
- **Nothing is sent**: HO-01's immediate count update and push notification to the senior on duty when somebody is marked urgent do not happen, and the screen says so at the head and again at the act.
- **Earlier handovers are listed as what they are missing** — never countersigned, never handed over, or neither — rather than under one label. Each is a different failure, and one word across all three would lose the worst inside the mildest.

## Incidents (Phase 6)

- **One harm vocabulary, four tiers, everywhere** (INC-01's badges say "HIGH HARM", which is not one of them). Already settled under Rulings on the PRD; the list's severity pills and the report form's harm question are both built from `INCIDENT_SEVERITIES`, so a fifth word has nowhere to come from.
- **"Not checked yet" and "Checked: no injury found" are two states, on the list as well as on the form** (INC-03). The first is hatched and says nobody has examined them; the second is settled, with who looked and when. Injuries found name their sites, because a count with no sites cannot be acted on.
- **The log is the home's, and nothing on it is counted over the viewer's list** (INC-01: care workers can view all incidents). An incident is an event in the building rather than a record about somebody on a list. Reporting one is a different act, and that one is asked of the role table resident by resident.
- **Each row is the whole record, and there is no "Open >"** (INC-01 gives every row one). The CW PRD specifies no screen for a single incident, so there is nowhere to open to, and a link to a screen that does not exist is the grey-link problem with a chevron on it. The row therefore carries what the record holds, the reporter's own words included: a log that shows a severity and hides what happened cannot make anybody aware of anything. The card says so where the link would have been.
- **Acknowledge, close and the CQC notification decision are drawn once, at the head of the list, with the role table's own words** (INC-01: care workers cannot do any of the three). Same treatment as the controlled drug register tab: visible that the act exists and is somebody else's, rather than hidden. **Acknowledging is drawn per row for a senior carer**, because it acts on one incident, and it says a manager closes it.
- **A CQC notification decision is refused for both roles.** INC-01 refuses the care worker and says nothing about a senior carer; Table 3's "Compliance and Reports" row gives neither role access, and a statutory notification is compliance. The act is in `capabilities.ts` sourced from INC-01 and that row together.
- **Nothing is sent, and the confirmation says so.** INC-03's confirmation is "Incident reported. Your manager has been notified." Nobody is notified: the screen says the incident waits on the list until a senior carer picks it up, and INC-01's cross-portal badge and push do not happen.
- **The form asks whether emergency services were called; the CW PRD does not.** `EmergencyServicesRecord` has two members and neither is an absence — calling is instantaneous, so there is no "not yet" — and a form that did not ask would write "nobody called an ambulance" in the reporter's name. The GP and the family are recorded as not yet contacted, which is what is true as the report is written, renders as unfinished, and can only under-claim.
- **The injury question is not asked where no resident was involved.** An equipment failure with nobody in it has nobody to examine, and asking would put "not checked yet" against a person who is not in the record.
- **"When it happened" is collected in the home's clock, not the reader's.** A `datetime-local` input is read in the viewer's zone by the browser, so the value is converted through `instantFromZonedWallClock`; the field says whose clock it is. A time in the future holds the form, and the loader refuses one.
- **A report is held for the session and says so**, like every other write in this build, and it is listed among what signing out would lose.

## Goals and activities (Phase 7)

- **The goals queue is the home's, and the act is asked per resident.** GOAL-01's card counts "across 40 at Rosewood Court", so the figures and the rows are the home's; adding a progress note asks the role table about that resident, and one off a care worker's list draws the PRD's question at the act. The care notes list is scoped the other way because CN-01 counts over the viewer's own residents: each screen follows its own words.
- **"Past its date and nothing said" counts every open goal past its date**, including one with progress notes on it, and the row says which. Ported from the Admin build with its reasoning: a trail of notes that stops short of an outcome is still nobody saying what happened. It is why the figure is 5 rather than the 4 a stricter reading gives.
- **A goal past its date is hatched; "the resident was not asked" is amber.** The first is a gap — nobody has recorded what happened — and the wait is the finding. The second is a compliance finding about how a decision was taken: somebody closed this person's goal without putting it to them, which is a thing that was done rather than a record nobody wrote.
- **"Resident not asked" holds closed goals only**, and never a withdrawal. An open goal has no closure to have asked about, and a withdrawal *is* the resident's view — the type says so, so the screen cannot ask it.
- **A progress note never changes a goal's outcome** (GOAL-02: status changes are a manager's). Setting and closing goals is drawn once at the head of the queue with the role table's refusal, not on every row.
- **Nothing is sent when a note is added.** GOAL-02's cross-portal note has the manager seeing a last-updated date and the Family Portal receiving a celebratory notification on a goal marked achieved. Neither happens: the Family Portal is not built, and the screen says so at the act.
- **The week is Monday to Sunday in the home's clock, with a week-at-a-time move.** ACT-01 asks for Week and Month; the month is not drawn. A month of sessions is a planning view and this product is what a shift works from — the same reasoning that keeps session planning itself out of this build. Calendar and List are two presentations of one week, so they are a segmented control (the first in the build, and the primitive the shape language has described since Phase 0).
- **A part-recorded session is not amber.** ACT-01 gives it an amber background; amber is for findings here, and the residents nobody has answered for are a gap. The card states both facts — "9 of 14 recorded" settled, "5 not recorded" hatched — rather than colouring the whole card for one of them. A session that has happened with nothing recorded takes the unrecorded treatment, which is ACT-01's dashed border by another name; a session still ahead is drawn plainly, because nothing is missing from it yet.
- **Nobody is answered for by leaving them alone.** Recording attendance writes only the residents somebody answered; the rest keep "nobody has said". A "did not attend" needs a reason, as a not-given dose does.
- **The engagement level is drawn and not kept** (ACT-02). `AttendanceState` has no field for it, and adding one is a change to data both products share — so the control is disabled with one line saying nothing is stored and why. The same for a note about the session as a whole: the shared record holds a note per uninvited joiner and none about the session.
- **Photo upload is drawn and stores nothing**, in the shape MED-04's PDF export and RES-02's call button already use. ACT-02's Family Portal photo feed and its push notification go nowhere: the Family Portal is not built.
- **People who joined uninvited are listed and are outside every figure**, with that said on the screen: the denominator is the invitation list, and adding them to it would rewrite the plan to say they were expected.
- **Creating, editing and cancelling a session are not built in this product.** Table 3 lets a senior carer create one, so the act is drawn with the line that it is not built here and is done in the Admin build; a care worker gets the table's refusal.
- **The resident record's Goals tab is now built**, and `LaterPhaseTab` is deleted with it: it was the last tab that said which phase would build it, and a component that throws for every input is not a placeholder any more.

## Senior carer records (Phase 8)

Four acts Table 3 gives a senior carer. RA-02 is the only one the CW PRD draws; the other three are adapted from the Admin build's screens, which is what the Scope departure above already settles.

- **Scoring a risk assessment is on each template a senior carer may score, and the refusal is drawn once** (RA-02, senior carers only). Scoring acts on one template, so for a reader who may, the control belongs on the row; a reader who may not meets the role table's reason once at the head rather than nine times down the list. A template this home does not carry out has no act at all: it is a question nobody here asks.
- **The running score is a total of what has been answered and says so** (RA-02). Until every factor has an answer the band is drawn as "band so far", hatched — a partial total presented as final would be a clinical band read off an incomplete instrument. The sign-off is held until every factor is answered, as RA-02 asks.
- **The placeholder banner is on the form as well as the list.** A scorer who meets the instrument here and nowhere else must still be told the weightings are invented.
- **Interventions are drawn and not kept** (RA-02 asks for free text, a responsible person and a review date). `RiskStatus` holds a level, a score, an author and a review date, and nothing about what anybody decided to do; adding a field is a change to data both products share. The form asks, and says at the act that nothing it collects is stored.
- **The band change is stated before the PIN goes in, and nothing is sent.** RA-02 pushes to every assigned care worker and notifies the manager when the band moves; neither happens, so the person signing is told what they have to say out loud instead. The badge on the resident's record does move, because that is this build's own state.
- **Recording a consent decision is on each type nobody has decided** (Table 3: "Consent — record"). A type that already has a decision carries no act: changing it means withdrawing what is there, which is a different act and is not built here. The capacity gate is the Admin build's, narrowed: both Mental Capacity Act stages are required where somebody lacks capacity, a best-interests decision needs somebody consulted and a reason, and the LPA holder is offered only where a health and welfare LPA is on record — a financial LPA cannot consent to care, and the screen says so rather than failing on submit.
- **The capacity assessment a decision carries names the one decision it was made about.** Blanket capacity is not expressible, which the mapped type holds at compile time.
- **Filing a document stores no file** (Table 3: "Documents — upload"). There is no server to put one on, so what is recorded is that the document exists and what is known about it, in the shape MED-04's export and RES-02's call button already use. The act stays at the head of the tab, because it is about the file rather than about any row.
- **The expiry question cannot be skipped, and "nobody knows" is one of its answers.** DOC-01's rule is that a document has an expiry decision only if it carries a date or somebody recorded that it does not expire. Asking makes the unknown-validity hatch something somebody chose rather than something a blank produced, and the form says what choosing it means.
- **Conducting a review can be completed over gaps, and the record carries which ones** (Table 3: "Reviews — conduct"). The handover signature's shape: refusing would mean the meeting happened and nothing holds evidence of it, and a review meeting happens *because* there are gaps. The screen names the outstanding domains rather than counting them.
- **What was discussed is not kept.** The review record holds who completed it, when, what it was against and which domains were outstanding, and no account of the meeting. Drawn, with one line saying so.
- **A resident nobody has scheduled a review for is in the queue, not outside it.** Nobody deciding when one is due is not the same as one not being due, and they sort after the dated ones rather than being given an invented position.
- **The review queue has no navigation entry.** The CW PRD names no Reviews module, so `/reviews` is reached from a resident's care plan rather than from the rail. Whether the navigation should carry one is a question for the PRD's author — the alternative is a screen the rail says nothing about.
- **Every one of the four refuses a care worker with the role table's words, drawn once**, and none of them is hidden: a hidden act says the record cannot be written rather than that it is not theirs to write.

## Dashboard (Phase 9)

- **No combined "Overdue now" figure**, as already recorded under Figures refused. The dark card counts the late things and states the three kinds beneath it — doses with no record, reviews past their date, handovers never countersigned — with one line saying why they are not added: a dose an hour late and a review four hundred days late are not one unit, and a handover belongs to a shift rather than to a resident.
- **"Due in the next 2 hours" is drawn as "Due now or in the next 2 hours"** (DASH-01, KPI 2). A round's window is an hour and the chart looks two hours ahead, so a dose that opened twenty minutes ago and one opening in ninety minutes are both work arriving in the reader's next two hours. Counting only the second put a zero on the card for most of the day, which reads as a screen that has stopped working rather than as a home with nothing coming. A dose whose window has closed is not in it: that is an omission, and it is counted as one.
- **Rounds are columns, not rings** (DASH-01a, "one ring per round"). Already recorded: the hatch is a repeating linear gradient and a gradient cannot follow a curve, so a ring would have to carry the gap in colour alone. The columns read the same records as the MAR chart.
- **The round time indicator is on every screen, not only the dashboard** (DASH-01). Every record in this build is generated against one instant, so the line saying which instant, and the link back to the real time, belong wherever a record is read. It has been in the shell since Phase 0 and the dashboard adds nothing to it.
- **The whole dashboard is scoped, not each figure.** Table 3's rule for this screen is the reach, so a care worker's residents are narrowed once and every figure counts over what is left; the head says "9 of 28 residents assigned to you", in DASH-01's own words. A care worker nobody has given a list is told that and shown no figures at all: the numbers would be the home's, and this screen is meant to be theirs.
- **An unacknowledged incident is drawn as a finding, apart from the hatch** (DASH-01's own text). It is the only bar with a third segment, and the reason is that somebody wrote the incident down: what is absent is a person picking it up, not a record.
- **Already late shows the twenty longest waiting**, and says where the rest are. A dashboard that listed every late thing would be the modules' queues again, and each of those screens already holds its own in full.
- **The quick action on a late row opens the record, never a new screen**: a late risk assessment opens the scoring form built in Phase 8, a late care plan review opens the review, a never-countersigned handover opens the handover, and a dose with no record opens the omissions list where a senior carer closes one.
